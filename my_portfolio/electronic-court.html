Electronic Court & Police Management System

Flutter Dart Firebase Auth Cloud Firestore Firebase Storage Cloud Messaging

A digital system that digitizes the legal process from initial case filing until resolution in court. It creates a shared database accessible to police and courts so every party stays informed throughout the process.

View on GitHub → All Projects

Overview

Objectives

  • Develop a digital system for easy creation, storage, retrieval and updating of case records.
  • Establish a central database accessible to different courts and police departments.
  • Enable citizens to track case progress and receive current legal updates.
  • Provide a citizen portal for direct communication between citizens and courts.

Key Users

  • Police
  • Prosecutors
  • Court Clerks
  • Judges
  • Citizens

Role-based Dashboards

Police, prosecutors, court staff, judges and lawyers each have dedicated dashboards tailored to their roles.

Police Features

  • Digital case filing
  • Digital case transfer to prosecutor
  • Digital case storage and tracking
  • Advanced search of offenders
  • Quick repeat-offender detection

Police can transfer cases to the prosecutor directly, the search unit, or prosecutor clerks. On submit, the system auto-detects if the suspect or victim appears in pending trial cases or probation records.

Citizen Features

  • View list of cases
  • Check case status
  • Track case journey
  • Receive up-to-date notifications

Citizens log in with email, password and national ID. The legal portal offers new and old laws, law firms and certified lawyers, departments that assist with legal issues, courts and court news, plus emergency help and legal chat.

Technologies

  • Frontend: Flutter, Dart
  • Backend: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Cloud Messaging
