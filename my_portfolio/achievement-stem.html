Award

1st Prize — RSTP STEM Expo 2025

Technology · Tertiary Category · Macmillan Education & Royal Science & Technology Park

Awarded first place for the Electronic Court and Police Management System at the national RSTP STEM Expo 2025 — a premier innovation exhibition hosted by the Royal Science & Technology Park in partnership with Macmillan Education.

The project digitizes the legal process from case filing through court resolution, connecting police, prosecutors, court clerks, judges, and citizens on one shared platform.

  • Category: Technology — Tertiary
  • Project: Electronic Court and Police Management System
  • Institution: Eswatini Medical Christian University
  • Hosts: RSTP · Macmillan Education Eswatini
